Box Jellyfish

Chironex fleckeri

Classification

Cnidarian

Aggressor or Retaliator

Aggressor – active hunter that uses tentacles to catch prey.

Feeding Behaviour

Feeds on small fish and prawns using venomous tentacles.

Breeding & Reproduction

Releases eggs and sperm into the water for external fertilisation.

Range & Habitat

Found in tropical northern Australian waters and Indo-Pacific oceans.

Labelled Diagram

World Distribution Map

Northern Australia, Philippines, Malaysia, and tropical Indo-Pacific waters.

Google Maps Habitat Screenshot

Why It Is Dangerous

Extremely painful sting that can cause heart failure.

First Aid & Safe Marine Practices

Pour vinegar over stings, remove tentacles carefully, and seek emergency medical help.

Great White Shark

Carcharodon carcharias

Classification

Fish (Cartilaginous Fish)

Aggressor or Retaliator

Aggressor – apex predator that actively hunts prey.

Feeding Behaviour

Feeds on fish, seals, sea lions, and marine mammals.

Breeding & Reproduction

Gives birth to live young after internal fertilisation.

Range & Habitat

Found in coastal and offshore waters around Australia and globally.

Labelled Diagram

World Distribution Map

Australia, South Africa, California, Mediterranean Sea, and temperate oceans worldwide.

Google Maps Habitat Screenshot

Why It Is Dangerous

Large size, strong bite force, and predatory behaviour can seriously injure humans.

First Aid & Safe Marine Practices

Control bleeding with pressure, call emergency services, and keep the injured person still.

Blue-Ringed Octopus

Hapalochlaena

Classification

Mollusc (Cephalopod)

Aggressor or Retaliator

Retaliator – usually shy and only bites when threatened or handled.

Feeding Behaviour

Feeds on small crabs, shrimp, and fish using venom to paralyse prey.

Breeding & Reproduction

Females lay eggs and protect them until hatching.

Range & Habitat

Found in tide pools, reefs, and shallow coastal waters around Australia and the Indo-Pacific.

Labelled Diagram

World Distribution Map

Australia, Japan, Indonesia, and Pacific coastal regions.

Google Maps Habitat Screenshot

Why It Is Dangerous

Highly venomous bite that can cause paralysis and breathing failure.

First Aid & Safe Marine Practices

Call emergency services immediately, apply pressure immobilisation bandage, and keep the person calm.
